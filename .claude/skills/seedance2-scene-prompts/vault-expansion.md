# Vault Expansion — 29 house setups in vault grammar

Original setups written for this project in the craft grammar distilled in
`vault-lessons.md`. They cover five domains the EL COLOGNE vault does not:
fragrance & beauty, UGC / creator frames, vertical (9:16) hook grammar, comic
timing, and Israeli street food. Use them exactly like `vault-library.md` —
swap the bracketed placeholders, or lift individual devices into house-format
shot lines.

Keep the two files distinct: `vault-library.md` is el_cologne's sourced
library; everything here is house-written and free to edit, extend, and
rewrite as the project's own material.

**AVOID key** (same as the library):
- *(none)* — no negative needed.
- **BASE** = `deformed bodies, extra limbs, morphing characters, warped faces,
  stutter-stepping, sliding feet, clipping through objects, floating weightless
  drift, on-screen text, watermarks, subtitles`
- **BASE +x** = BASE plus the listed extension terms.

Two notes on how negatives are assigned here, both following
`vault-lessons.md` §2:

1. Nearly half these setups carry **no negative** — static talking heads,
   hands-only food macros, and locked tableaux have no character-motion or
   physics risk to suppress.
2. **Drop the negative when one of its terms would fight a deliberate
   effect.** "Kitchen Counter Demo" wants the wide-lens face distortion of a
   real lean-in, so `warped faces` would work against the shot. A negative
   that contradicts the intent costs more than it saves.

## Fragrance & Beauty Cinema — 7 setups

### Atomizer Cloud at 1000fps  [100mm · Backlit]
AVOID: BASE +unnatural liquid physics
> 1000fps locked macro on [BOTTLE]'s atomizer as a single press releases a cone of mist against pure black. Backlight resolves the cloud into thousands of individual droplets, each a lit point that slows, hangs, and begins to fall as one weather system. The bottle's glass edge holds a hard rim highlight; nothing else exists in frame. 100mm macro, museum-grade stillness underneath the explosion of mist. No music — the press, the hiss, then nothing. Single continuous take; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Amber in the Glass  [85mm · Windowsill]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Slow 180° turn of [BOTTLE] on a windowsill in late afternoon sun. The amber liquid catches the light and throws a caustic pattern across white linen, and the pattern stretches and slides as the bottle turns. Dust motes drift in the beam. 85mm, shallow focus held on the engraved name. The caustic is the protagonist; the bottle is only its instrument. Quiet room tone, a curtain moving once.

### Skin and Silk First Light  [65mm · Dawn]
AVOID: BASE
> Dawn light through gauze curtains finds [MODEL]'s collarbone as they press two fingers of fragrance to the pulse point — the gesture unhurried, practiced, private. 65mm close, focus breathing from the wrist up to a small smile that isn't for anyone. True skin texture, no retouching gloss. The ritual is the luxury; the product never fills the frame. Linen sounds, a far city waking.

### The Bottle as Architecture  [50mm · Studio]
AVOID: *(none)*
> [BOTTLE] shot like a building at blue hour: a slow, low orbit looking up its faceted glass, each pane catching a different band of a gradient cyc — indigo into amber. Edges read as cornices, the cap as a roofline. 50mm from below, perspective compressed until scale turns ambiguous. One soft light sweep crosses the facade mid-orbit. Premium, geometric, unhurried.

### Counter Test Moment  [40mm · Retail]
AVOID: BASE
> Handheld 40mm over the shoulder at a department-store counter: [SUBJECT] sprays a test strip, waves it once, brings it to their face. The micro-performance carries everything — eyes closing a half-second before the strip arrives, a small involuntary nod, the glance at the price they pretend not to take. White store bokeh and mirrors behind. The decision happens in the nod, not in words.

### Night-Out Vanity  [35mm · Tungsten]
AVOID: BASE
> Locked mirror shot in a small apartment bathroom before a night out: [SUBJECT] in a half-zipped dress, music muffled from a phone speaker on the sink. Two sprays — wrist, neck — then a beat of looking at themselves that has nothing to do with fragrance. Warm tungsten bulb over the mirror, one flicker. The armor goes on last and weighs nothing. Phone-speaker bass, a zipper, silence.

### Molecule Garden  [Probe macro · Black]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Probe-lens journey through a dark field of suspended botanicals — bergamot peel curling, a split vanilla pod, iris petals frozen mid-fall — each ingredient lit like a museum specimen as the lens glides between them, almost touching. At the path's end the ingredients blur into the amber of [BOTTLE] waiting in the dark. The formula is walked through, not listed. Deep quiet; one glass note when the bottle appears.

## UGC / Creator Frames — 6 setups

### Front-Camera Honesty  [Phone front cam · Ring light]
AVOID: BASE
> Vertical front-camera framing, slightly too close the way real selfie videos are: [CREATOR] holds the phone in one hand, walks and talks, the image micro-shaking with each step, autofocus breathing once. Ring-light catchlights double in their eyes when they stop at the mirror. One-take energy; a laugh interrupts a sentence and gets kept. The imperfection is the trust. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular.

### Kitchen Counter Demo  [28mm · Daylight]
AVOID: *(none)* — `warped faces` would fight the intentional wide-lens lean-in
> Phone propped against the toaster: [CREATOR] demos [PRODUCT] on a genuinely cluttered kitchen counter — a kid's cup, unopened mail. They talk to the lens while the hands work, and when the result lands they lean in until the face slightly distorts in the wide lens. Window daylight, no fill. Cabinet doors, tap water, an off-screen call from another room they wave away without looking.

### Package Opening, Real Time  [Overhead clamp · Desk lamp]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Overhead phone clamp above a desk: two hands open [PACKAGE] in true real time — tape resisting, a box flap tearing slightly, packing paper everywhere. No jump cuts; the fumbling stays. The reveal gets four full seconds of silence before the first word. Warm desk-lamp pool, everything else dim. ASMR-adjacent: tape, cardboard, tissue, breath.

### Car Seat Confession  [Front cam · Parked]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Parked-car front-camera frame, seatbelt still on: [CREATOR] talks straight into the lens, daylight through the windshield doing the key. Mid-thought they look off through the side window, then back — the edit-point look that never gets edited out. Rain starts on the roof halfway through and they just talk over it. The car is the only room where nobody interrupts.

### Get Ready With Me, Left Third  [Mirror lean · LED strip]
AVOID: BASE
> Phone leaning on a vanity mirror so [CREATOR] occupies the left third while their reflection does the work on the right: brushes, hair clips opening, [PRODUCT] used mid-sentence without a pitch. LED-strip glow mixed with one warm bulb, honest and uncorrected. They lose the thread of the story and find it again. The routine is the content; the talking is the friendship. The subject and their reflection stay one consistent person throughout.

### Duet-Ready Reaction Wall  [Locked vertical · Bedroom]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Locked vertical frame against a bedroom wall built to be dueted: [CREATOR] centered from the waist up, poster edges and a hanging plant framing them symmetrically. They react in the honest register — a real pause to think before speaking, eyebrows arriving before the words. One lamp key, window fill. The empty half of the story is the invitation.

## Vertical Hook Grammar (9:16) — 6 setups

### The Interrupted Action Hook  [Vertical · Any]
AVOID: BASE
> The clip opens mid-action with zero establishment — [SUBJECT]'s hand already inside the machine, the sauce already dripping, the fall already happening — one full second before any context arrives. Only then does the camera pull back to reveal what we're inside. The hook is the confusion; the reveal is the reward. Hard cut in, no fade, no logo. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Text-Free Thumb-Stop  [Vertical · Static open]
AVOID: *(none)*
> A vertical frame engineered to stop a thumb without a single word on screen: one impossible-but-real image held perfectly static for the first 1.5 seconds — a violin on a fishing boat, a wedding dress at a car wash — before any motion begins. Stillness against a feed full of motion is the pattern interrupt. Then one slow push. Nothing explains itself early.

### Loop Seam Walk  [28mm · Corridor]
AVOID: BASE
> Vertical loop: [SUBJECT] walks toward camera down a corridor and, at the end of the frame, pushes a door that opens onto the corridor's own first frame — position, light, and stride matched so the last frame IS the first. Viewers realize on the second lap. 28mm, constant walking tempo, fluorescent fixtures passing in rhythm. Built as a seamless loop; characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Cost Reveal Ladder  [Top-down inserts · One table]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Three-beat vertical escalation in one location: the same table set three times in match-framed cuts — paper plates, then ceramic, then linen and candles — hands swapping items in fast top-down moves between beats. Each beat holds exactly long enough to register one price class. The ladder is the story; no numbers appear. Snappy object sounds; music only on beat three.

### Half-Second Before Disaster  [Locked · Real physics]
AVOID: BASE
> The clip opens on the half-second BEFORE the obvious disaster — the tray tilting, the dog noticing the cake — and freezes there one beat longer than comfortable while nothing happens. Then it happens. The withheld beat is the hook; the payoff is honest, with no cut cheating the impact. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Comment-Bait Choice Frame  [Locked · Natural light]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Vertical split staging inside one real frame: two options physically side by side — two plates, two outfits on the bed, two literal paths at a fork — camera locked while a hand enters, hovers between them, and withdraws without choosing. The unchosen choice is the engagement engine. Natural light, no graphics; the frame itself asks the question.

## Comic Timing — 5 setups

### The Deadpan Hold  [Static medium · Flat light]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Something mildly absurd happens to [SUBJECT] — the umbrella inverts, the automatic door refuses only them — and the comedy is assigned entirely to the hold afterward: four full seconds of them looking at the thing, then almost at the lens, then not. No music sting, no zoom, nothing underlined. The joke is the duration.

### Rule of Three Errands  [Match-framed · Doorway]
AVOID: BASE
> Three match-framed cuts of the same doorway: [SUBJECT] exits with one bag and returns; exits with four bags and returns; exits pulling an entire refrigerator on a hand truck and does not return. Identical framing, identical walk tempo — only the object escalates. The third beat holds two beats longer than the first two. Flat afternoon light, deadpan world. Characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Background Betrayal  [Locked MCU · Soft background]
AVOID: BASE
> [SUBJECT] delivers something earnest in a locked medium close-up while, in the soft background, a second story quietly ruins them — movers carrying their couch away, a pigeon dismantling their sandwich. Focus never racks back; the background stays soft but legible. The speaker never learns. The comedy lives behind the sincerity. Characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### The Over-Prepared Entrance  [Low tracking · Glass doors]
AVOID: BASE
> A tracking shot builds an epic entrance — slow motion, wind in the jacket, low angle on [SUBJECT] striding toward glass doors — that ends with the door being a pull, not a push. Real weight on the stopped body. The film grammar promises a hero; the door files a correction. Slow motion dies to real time on contact. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Sync Fail Duo  [Two-shot · Café]
AVOID: BASE
> [SUBJECT A] and [SUBJECT B] attempt one simple synchronized action — a handshake-to-hug, a high five across a table — and miss into a compounding negotiation of elbows and apologies, played completely straight, never to camera. Café clatter continues, indifferent. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular; grounded, stable physics throughout.

## Street Food Heat — 5 setups

### Shawarma Knife Cascade  [50mm · Stand]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Tight 50mm on the shawarma spit at full glow: the long knife rides the cone in one practiced stroke and shavings cascade into the waiting laffa held below, steam rising through frame. The cook's forearm and the knife know each other; nothing is hurried. Gas roar, knife hiss, the rattle of the metal tray. Appetite built from heat, motion, and sound — no plating, no garnish.

### Laffa Steam Open  [Probe macro · Counter]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Probe-lens dive toward a just-wrapped laffa as two hands tear it in half — the pull of the bread, tahini threading between the halves, steam unloading upward through backlight. The lens ends almost inside the tear. One motion, given full slow-motion respect. The tear is the money shot; everything else waits its turn.

### Grill Line at Rush  [28mm handheld · Night market]
AVOID: BASE
> Handheld 28mm slides down a market grill line at Friday rush: skewers turning in ranks, flame flaring when fat drops, the cook's tongs conducting three conversations at once. Faces arrive lit from below by coals. The camera gets jostled and keeps its subject anyway — vérité appetite. Orders shouted in shorthand, coals breathing. Single continuous take; characters stay consistent and singular.

### Market Juice Crush  [40mm · Tarp daylight]
AVOID: BASE +unnatural liquid physics
> Locked 40mm on a market juice stand: pomegranate halves pressed in the long-armed crusher, ruby juice sheeting into the pitcher in one continuous pour, pulp confetti across steel. The vendor's rhythm is the metronome — press, turn, press. Hard daylight through tarp stripes paints the steel red and gold. Sticky, loud, alive. Single continuous take; grounded, stable physics throughout.

### Sabich Assembly Line  [Top-down locked · Counter]
AVOID: *(none)*
> Top-down locked frame on one pita held open in a palm: the build arrives in strict order — fried eggplant, an egg halved in one knife-press, salad by the handful, amba ribboned in a figure eight, tahini last in a spiral. Each addition lands with its own sound. The order IS the recipe; the hand never leaves frame. No music — the counter is the percussion.
