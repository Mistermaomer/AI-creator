# Worked example

Illustrative example — match this density and tone, never copy its content.

**Brief given by the user**: "20-second night-market chase. Two images attached:
first is my character, second is the location." → 2 segments, `@Image 1` = character,
`@Image 2` = location, no optional anchors.

**Delivered prompt block**:

```
PART 1 · Keep it locked in for one entire scene.

[CHAR A]: @Image 1 A wiry man in his early thirties, average height, olive skin with a faint scar through his left eyebrow, short black hair damp with sweat, dark stubble. Wears a creased grey button-down with rolled sleeves, untucked over black trousers, worn leather shoes. Moves fast but economical — a man used to running, eyes always checking behind him.

[LOCATION]: @Image 2 A narrow night-market alley — low canvas awnings strung with bare tungsten bulbs, steam rising from food stalls, wet stone underfoot reflecting warm light. Crowded shelving and hanging goods press the walkway tight; light enters in warm pools between long stretches of shadow. Air thick with smoke and drizzle.

[LOOK]: Shot on 35mm film, hyper-realistic, photographic realism with fine natural grain and true skin texture and imperfection. Deep shadow and contrast, shallow depth of field, soft out-of-focus elements in the foreground. Desaturated tones, a deliberate, evocative cinematic color palette. A real film still, not a render.
Avoid: CGI sheen, plastic smooth skin, glowing eyes, neon VFX, video-game look.

CONTINUITY RULES (apply to every shot — fill with what's constant for this project):

Pace / tempo: Urgent but readable — quick cuts, each shot holds just long enough to land.

Weight & consequence: Fully physical — stalls shake when clipped, puddles splash, hanging lamps swing after he passes.

Scale of motion: Grounded, human — no superhuman leaps; he stumbles, shoulders through, recovers.

What to cut to: Hands grabbing surfaces, vendors' startled faces, feet on wet stone, the dark mouth of the alley ahead.

Progression over the scene: The market thins and darkens as he runs — from crowded warm light into empty blue-black shadow.

Recurring camera motif: A low tracking shot at knee height surging forward — once per segment, on the key beat.

═ PART 2 · SEGMENT (the 3 shots — fill these in) ═

SEGMENT 01 — :00 – :10 · INTO THE CROWD

Shot 1A (mid, ~3s): [CHAR A] bursts through a plastic curtain into [LOCATION], knocking a crate edge, handheld camera whipping to follow him into the crowd. [LOOK]. Audio: curtain snap, crate scrape, market chatter swelling.

Shot 1B (close, ~3s): [CHAR A]'s face over his shoulder as he weaves — breath ragged, eyes flicking back, steam drifting between him and the lens. [LOOK]. Audio: hard breathing, sizzling stalls, a distant shout behind him.

Shot 1C (tracking, ~4s): Low knee-height tracking shot surging forward through the crowd as [CHAR A] shoulders past vendors, a swinging lamp raking light across the wet stone. [LOOK]. Audio: pounding footsteps on wet stone, jostled metal pans, the shout closer now.

SEGMENT 02 — :10 – :20 · THE DARK END

Shot 2A (wide, ~3s): The market thins — [CHAR A] a small figure sprinting from the last pool of warm stall light into the darkening far end of [LOCATION], static camera letting him cross the frame. [LOOK]. Audio: chatter fading behind, footsteps echoing longer, drizzle on canvas.

Shot 2B (close, ~3s): [CHAR A] flattens against a shuttered stall in near-dark, chest heaving, rain tracing the scar through his eyebrow as he listens. [LOOK]. Audio: his held breath, dripping water, one set of slow footsteps approaching.

Shot 2C (tracking, ~4s): Knee-height tracking shot gliding past the shuttered stall — [CHAR A] slips out of frame into the blue-black mouth of the alley, leaving only swinging shadow. [LOOK]. Audio: a single splash of a puddle step, then silence under the rain.
```

**After the block, in the user's language**: image legend (image 1 = `@Image 1` the
character, image 2 = `@Image 2` the location), reminder to attach the images to
Seedance in that order, and an offer to revise a segment, change the length, or swap
an anchor.
