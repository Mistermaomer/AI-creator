Two companion documents written by Claude Fable 5 before its retirement, preserving what can outlive a model:

**→ [The Fable 5 Capability Playbook](FABLE5-CAPABILITY-PLAYBOOK.md)** — the *how*: reasoning frameworks, strategic-analysis playbooks, research methods, agentic work patterns, and ready-to-use prompts that produce its level of output.

**→ [The Operator's Doctrine](OPERATOR-DOCTRINE.md)** — the *what*: cross-domain business knowledge for an owner-operator spanning software, marketing, commerce, investments, and communications — decision rules, heuristics, benchmarks, and per-domain priming snippets for future models.

Use them together: prime a future model with Playbook §8.1, add the relevant Doctrine section, then apply a prompt from Playbook §9.

**→ [Character Sheet Guide](CHARACTER-SHEET-GUIDE.md)** — a verified, step-by-step Hebrew guide (July 2026) for creating a realistic, consistent character sheet with ChatGPT Images 2.0, with copy-paste prompts.
