```
PART 1 · Keep it locked in for one entire scene.

[CHAR A]: A head midwife in her mid-forties — sturdy build, warm olive skin with true texture and laugh lines, dark curls escaping a teal surgical cap, brown eyes that command a room. Wears teal scrubs with a hospital badge and a stethoscope around her neck, surgical mask up during the corridor run and pulled down at the reveal. Moves like a professional under pressure, but her urgency is joyful — the thrill of good news, a smile visible in her eyes even above the mask; melts into happy, tearful tenderness at the end.

[PROP]: @Image 1 The white 2-liter Sano Maxima BABY laundry-gel bottle exactly as photographed — soft rounded shoulders, built-in side handle, ribbed matte amber-yellow cap, cream label with the red Sano logo, the Maxima brand block, a photo of a baby in a knitted yellow bonnet and pale-blue BABY lettering. The printed label is reproduced precisely from the photograph, never redrawn or altered. Roughly the size and weight of a swaddled newborn; through Segment 01 it stays wrapped in white muslin cloth, unrecognizable.

[EXTRAS]: A four-person delivery team in matching teal scrubs, caps and masks — two pushing the gurney, one sprinting ahead to slam doors open, one steadying the swinging IV pole. They read excited and half-blurred at the frame edges, grinning under their masks like a team rushing to deliver good news, and they stay in scrubs even inside the supermarket — the incongruity is deliberate.

[LOCATION]: One continuous run: a hospital delivery-ward corridor — pale mint walls, scuffed linoleum, cold fluorescent ceiling strips, banks of double swing doors — that transforms mid-run, past the final doors, into a supermarket laundry aisle keeping the same corridor geometry: tall stocked shelves of detergent bottles where the walls were, polished floor, warm golden light replacing the fluorescence, and one empty, spotlit gap on the middle shelf waiting like a bassinet.

[LOOK]: Shot on 35mm film, hyper-realistic, photographic realism with fine natural grain and true skin texture and imperfection. Soft, bright contrast, shallow depth of field, soft out-of-focus elements in the foreground. Lightly desaturated tones, a warm, uplifting cinematic color palette. A real film still, not a render.
Avoid: CGI sheen, plastic smooth skin, glowing eyes, neon VFX, video-game look.

CONTINUITY RULES (apply to every shot — fill with what's constant for this project):

Pace / tempo: One long deceleration — Segment 01 cuts at a sprint, every shot urgent but joyful, the adrenaline of good news and never fear; Segment 02 eases into soft slow motion; no neutral shots anywhere.

Weight & consequence: Fully physical — swing doors bang and rebound, the IV bag whips, gurney wheels squeal on linoleum, shelf bottles tremble as the gurney glides past.

Scale of motion: Grounded and human — real running, real momentum; nothing floats except the oat flakes drifting down in the final shots.

What to cut to: Gloved hands firm on the gurney rail, bright excited eyes above masks, the swinging IV bag, strobing ceiling lights, the empty spotlit shelf gap, [PROP]'s label.

Progression over the scene: Bright mint-and-fluorescent commotion dissolves into warm golden calm — the monitor beeping becomes a music-box lullaby, the sprint becomes slow motion, hospital walls become supermarket shelves without a single cut.

Recurring camera motif: A gurney-height forward surge through a threshold — the swing doors in Segment 01, the spotlit shelf gap in Segment 02 — once per segment, on the key beat.

═ PART 2 · SEGMENT (the 3 shots — fill these in) ═

SEGMENT 01 — :00 – :10 · THE RUSH

Shot 1A (POV, ~3s): Lying patient's-eye POV from the gurney racing down the corridor of [LOCATION] — ceiling fluorescent strips strobing across the upper frame, chrome side rails running along both frame edges, the swaddled [PROP] rising low at the bottom of frame exactly where a laboring belly would be, reading as one at first glance, no legs, feet or any body on the mattress — [CHAR A] leaning in over the lens, gripping the rail and shouting ahead, [EXTRAS] bursting a set of swing doors open toward the camera; all hospital here, no hint of the store yet. [LOOK]. Audio: quick upbeat monitor beeping with no alarms, clattering gurney wheels, a joyful muffled shout: "She's coming — clear the corridor!"

Shot 1B (close, ~3s): Whip-cut excitement details — [CHAR A]'s smiling, excited eyes above her mask, gloved hands firm on the rail, the IV bag swinging hard, [EXTRAS]' sneakers pounding the scuffed linoleum. [LOOK]. Audio: the beeping quickening brightly, quick excited breaths, a nurse's short laugh between shouts.

Shot 1C (tracking, ~4s): Gurney-height tracking surge from behind as the team slams through the final double doors, hands gripping the rails, the swaddled [PROP] a small white bundle tucked on the mattress — and only past the doors [LOCATION] begins to turn: mint tiles dissolving into stocked shelving, cold light warming to gold deep down the aisle while the foreground stays hospital. [LOOK]. Audio: doors banging open, the beeping peaking, one soft warm chime underneath.

SEGMENT 02 — :10 – :20 · BORN ON THE SHELF

Shot 2A (wide, ~3s): Full reveal — the gurney glides out of the last cold light into the golden supermarket aisle of [LOCATION], the sprint of [EXTRAS] melting into slow motion, the empty spotlit shelf gap waiting ahead like a bassinet. [LOOK]. Audio: the beeping slowing, softening into the first bars of a music-box lullaby, wheels hushing on polished floor.

Shot 2B (close, ~4s): [CHAR A] pulls her mask down, lifts the muslin-wrapped [PROP] from the gurney with both hands and nestles it into the spotlit gap, unwrapping the cloth from its label like presenting a newborn, her eyes wet with joy; oat flakes drift down through the spotlight like the gentlest snow. [LOOK]. Audio: the lullaby blooming, a tiny newborn coo, a gasp from [EXTRAS].

Shot 2C (mid, ~3s): [EXTRAS] break into applause and happy tears as the camera makes one slow gurney-height push through the aisle onto [PROP] glowing on the shelf, muslin fallen open at its base, label immaculate. [LOOK]. Audio: warm applause, the lullaby resolving, one soft heartbeat, then silence.
```

## תוכנית ריצה לווידאו (אחרי אישור פריימי הפריוויו)

שני הפריימים המאושרים משמשים עוגני start_image — זה נועל את הלוק שאושר.

**Run A — סגמנט 1 (10 שניות):**
1. תמונות: פקשוט נקי של הבקבוק ראשון (= `@Image 1`) + פריים ההוק המאושר בתור **start_image**.
2. פרומפט: PART 1 המלא + SEGMENT 01 בלבד (למחוק את שורות סגמנט 02).
3. הגדרות: 9:16 · std · 10 שניות. להריץ 2-3 טייקים.

**Run B — סגמנט 2 (10 שניות):**
1. לחלץ את הפריים האחרון מהטייק הנבחר של Run A (צילום מסך ברזולוציה מלאה / ייצוא פריים ב-CapCut) — הוא ה-**start_image** של Run B. (חלופה אם התפר לא יושב: פריים המורף המאושר.)
2. תמונות: פקשוט (= `@Image 1`) + הפריים שחולץ כ-start_image.
3. פרומפט: PART 1 המלא + SEGMENT 02 בלבד. אותן הגדרות, 2-3 טייקים.

(אם הפלטפורמה מאפשרת 20 שניות בריצה אחת: כל הפרומפט בבת אחת, start_image = פריים ההוק. פחות שליטה, פחות תפרים.)

**QC לכל טייק:** התווית של [PROP] נאמנה למקור? אין גוף/רגליים על האלונקה? הצוות שמח ולא מבוהל? ב-1A אין שום רמז לחנות? ב-1C המורף מתחיל רק אחרי הדלתות? ידיים על האלונקה כל הזמן?

**פוסט (CapCut):**
1. חיבור A+B — החתך על פריצת הדלתות מסתיר את התפר.
2. כתוביות עברית כשכבת עריכה בלבד — "בשעה טובה!" בשיא שוט 2B, ו"נולד BABY חדש על המדף" + לוגו סנו על שוט 2C. לא לבקש טקסט עברי מהמודל — הוא משבש טיפוגרפיה עברית.
3. הקריאה "She's coming — clear the corridor!" באנגלית לצורכי הג'נרוט; להשאיר סאונד-דיזיין בלבד או לדבב לעברית ("היא בדרך! פנו את המסדרון!") בפוסט.
4. אם האודיו המג'ונרט חלש: ביפ מוניטור + שיר ערש קופסת נגינה מספריות. אופציונלי: אפסקייל 4K.

## פרומפטים לתמונות פריוויו (מודל תמונה)

לבדיקת הקונספט ויזואלית לפני ריצת וידאו. שניהם 9:16.

**פריים 1 — ההוק: POV מהאלונקה (שוט 1A), גרסה 2.** נבנה סביב גיאומטריית שכיבה אמיתית: תקרה שולטת בפריים, מעקות כרום משני הצדדים, פנים רוכנות מעל העדשה, והצרור צמוד לעדשה בתחתית — קריא כבטן. חובה: 100% בית חולים, אפס סופרמרקט (הטוויסט נשרף אחרת):

```
Low first-person POV photograph shot from a camera lying flat on a hospital gurney at pillow height, lens tilted up about 30 degrees, racing down a delivery-ward corridor. True lying-patient composition: the ceiling with bright fluorescent light strips fills the upper half of the frame, motion-blurred and streaking backward; the gurney's chrome side rails run along the left and right edges of the frame, converging toward the feet; at the very bottom edge, close to the lens and softly out of focus, one rounded bundle wrapped in white muslin rises under the taut sheet — foreshortened so it reads exactly like the patient's own sheet-covered pregnant belly seen from the pillow. A midwife in her mid-forties in teal scrubs, surgical cap and mask leans in over the rail directly above the camera, mid-shout, her eyes smiling and excited; a second nurse pushes at the foot of the gurney, half-blurred; ahead, double swing doors burst open. Everything is hospital: pale mint walls, cold-white fluorescent light, scuffed linoleum — no store, no shelves, no warm golden light. Joyful, high-energy rush — the adrenaline of good news, not fear. Shot on 35mm film, hyper-realistic photographic realism, fine natural grain, true skin texture, shallow depth of field, slight handheld shake, lightly desaturated mint palette. A real film still, not a render. Vertical 9:16 frame.
```

**פריים 2 — פריים המפתח: מאחורי האלונקה, רגע המורף (שוט 1C), גרסה 2.** חצי-חצי בכפייה: הקדמה עדיין בית חולים, הטרנספורמציה קורית באמצע הפריים, ידיים על האלונקה:

```
Cinematic photograph from directly behind a hospital gurney at gurney height, racing away from the camera as a medical team pushes it through burst-open double swing doors. Clear composition split: the near half of the frame is still hospital — pale mint walls, scuffed linoleum, cold white fluorescent light around the door frames; beyond the doors the same corridor is caught mid-transformation into a supermarket laundry aisle — mint tiles visibly dissolving into tall shelves stocked with white laundry-detergent bottles, the light shifting into a warm golden glow deeper down the aisle, one empty spotlit gap waiting on a middle shelf in the distance. Two medics in teal scrubs grip the gurney rails and push, leaning into the run; two more sprint alongside, half-blurred, joyful energy in their body language. On the mattress lies only one small rounded bundle swaddled in white muslin, tucked under the edge of the taut sheet — no patient. Shot on 35mm film, hyper-realistic photographic realism, fine natural grain, soft bright contrast, shallow depth of field, motion blur on the running figures, lightly desaturated palette warming from mint in the foreground to gold in the distance. A real film still, not a render. Vertical 9:16 frame.
```

**Negative prompt לפריים 1 (ההוק):** `supermarket, store shelves, products, warm golden light, camera behind the gurney, third-person view, feet, legs, knees, toes, patient body, person lying on the gurney, patient face, pregnant woman, baby, mummy, visible text, captions, CGI sheen, plastic smooth skin`

**Negative prompt לפריים 2 (המורף):** `patient lying on the gurney, body or legs under the sheet, pregnant woman, baby, mummy, unattended gurney, fully transformed supermarket, supermarket in the foreground, pharmacy shelves, cosmetics, wall posters with faces, visible text, captions, CGI sheen, plastic smooth skin`

שים לב: בפריים 2 **אין** בנגטיב `feet, legs` הכלליים — הרגליים של הצוות הרץ חייבות להיראות; הנגטיב מכוון רק נגד גוף של מטופל על האלונקה.

**חוקי ברזל לפריים ההוק (מלקחי איטרציה ראשונה):** (1) אסור שיופיע שום אלמנט של חנות — הטוויסט נשרף; (2) המצלמה שוכבת על האלונקה, לא עומדת מאחוריה — אם רואים את האלונקה מבחוץ, הצרור נראה כמו חפץ מוזר במקום בטן; (3) חייב להיות איש צוות אחד לפחות דוחף/רוכן מעל האלונקה — אלונקה "נוסעת לבד" נראית כמו סרט אימה.
