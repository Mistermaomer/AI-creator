## 1. Concept & Production Notes

**Logline.** High noon in a sun-bleached frontier town. Lionel Messi, the weathered veteran gunslinger, faces Lamine Yamal, the young challenger, in a classic spaghetti-western standoff. Both draw. Neither wins. The camera rises to reveal what they were really dueling over: the World Cup trophy, gleaming on a post between them.

**Style reference.** Sergio Leone, *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly* — the final Triello: extreme close-ups on eyes, hands hovering over holsters, editing that accelerates toward the draw, vast empty wides, oppressive heat and silence.

**Technical spec (Instagram Reels).**

- Canvas: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920
- Picture: 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen letterboxed inside the vertical frame — black bars top and bottom. The cinematic bars ARE part of the look; the image band sits at vertical center
- Duration: 15.0 seconds exactly
- Frame rate feel: 24fps cinematic cadence with visible film grain
- Keep all critical imagery inside the letterboxed band; no essential detail in the top 15% or bottom 20% of the canvas (Reels UI zones)

**Color grade.** Warm desert western: burnt yellow-brown dust, bleached near-white hot sky, deep sunburnt skin tones, faded costume colors. Heavy 35mm film grain, subtle anamorphic lens distortion and oval bokeh, slight gate weave. Highlights bloom warm; shadows are dusty brown, never pure black (except the letterbox bars).

**Wardrobe & characters.**

- **Messi** — the veteran: short grey-flecked beard, worn brown leather poncho with faint sky-blue-and-white striped serape pattern (Argentina echo), dark flat-brimmed hat low over the eyes, aged gun belt
- **Yamal** — the challenger: young face, defiant grin, dusty red-and-gold trimmed poncho (Spain echo), no hat so the wind moves his hair, newer gun belt worn low
- Both wear period revolvers in leather holsters; the styling is clearly costumed western fantasy, not realism

**Likeness fallback.** If the generator blocks real-person likenesses, replace names with: "a legendary veteran Argentine footballer-gunslinger in his late 30s, short beard, calm heavy-lidded eyes" and "a teenage Spanish football prodigy-gunslinger, sharp youthful features, fearless eyes." Keep every other detail of every shot unchanged.

## 3. MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY

1. **LEONE EXTREME CLOSE-UP (used 5x)** — Shots 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 — eyes and hands filling the full widescreen band; the film's core visual language
2. **HEAT SHIMMER / HAZE (used 4x)** — Shots 1, 2, 3, 7 — atmospheric distortion selling desert heat and dreamlike tension
3. **SNAP-CUT RHYTHM (used across Shots 1→2, 4→5, and all of 6)** — hard cuts timed to bell tolls; cut rate is the tension instrument
4. **ACCELERATING MONTAGE (used 1x)** — Shot 6 — cuts compress 0.5s → 0.25s → 4 frames with stepped zoom tightening; the Triello engine
5. **TOTAL-STILLNESS BEAT (used 1x)** — Shot 7 — suspended dust, zero camera movement, sound drop-out; negative-space effect before the climax
6. **EXTREME SLOW MOTION (used 1x)** — Shot 8 — ~15-20% speed on the simultaneous draw, with a mirrored match cut joining both men into one motion
7. **FREEZE-FRAME + PARALLAX DRIFT (used 1x)** — Shot 9 — world frozen one frame before the trigger, camera drifting through frozen space — the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT
8. **FROZEN-TIME CRANE REVEAL (used 1x)** — Shot 10 — rise and pull-back inside the frozen instant to reveal the trophy on its post
9. **ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARE (used 1x)** — Shot 10 — hard golden bloom off the trophy
10. **TUMBLEWEED FOREGROUND WIPE (used 1x)** — Shot 3 — practical-feeling frame wipe inside the establishing wide
11. **FILM GRAIN / GATE WEAVE / 2.39:1 LETTERBOX (constant)** — every frame — the cinematic package that reads "movie" instantly on a phone screen
12. **TITLE-CARD STAMP (used 1x)** — Shot 11 — three-beat distressed type, single bell, fade to loopable black

## 5. ENERGY ARC

- **Act 1 — The Stare (00:00-00:04):** The hook is intimacy, not spectacle — a stranger's eyes filling a phone screen stop the scroll. Two matched ECUs establish the rivalry wordlessly; the wide shot answers "where am I" exactly when the viewer asks it.
- **Act 2 — The Standoff (00:04-00:09):** Pure Leone mechanics: the montage compresses until the cuts are almost subliminal, then slams into total silence. The silence at 00:07.5 is the arc's fulcrum — the loudest moment in the film is the one with nothing in it.
- **Act 3 — The Draw & The Prize (00:09-00:15):** The explosion arrives in slow motion and is refused its resolution: the freeze one frame before the trigger denies a winner forever. The energy converts from violence to awe as the camera rises to the trophy — the duel was never about each other. The title card lands the joke and the black end-frame hands the loop back to Messi's eyes.
