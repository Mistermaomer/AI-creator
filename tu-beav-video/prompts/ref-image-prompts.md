# פרומפטים לרפרנסים החסרים

שני רפרנסים חסרים לג'נרציה 3 (הדלת + המלונה): האישה, והבית עם המלונה והכלב.
הפרומפטים כתובים כך שהתוצאה תתאים בסגנון לקרקטר-שיטים הקיימים (פיקסאר תלת-ממד, רקע סטודיו אפור-כחלחל, שורת זוויות + שורת הבעות). מומלץ לצרף את הקרקטר-שיט של הבחור כרפרנס סגנון בעת היצירה.

## פרומפט A — קרקטר-שיט: האישה

```
Pixar-style 3D animated character reference sheet, matching the exact rendering
style, lighting, and neutral gray-blue studio background of the attached
reference sheet. Character: an Israeli woman in her early 30s, warm pretty
face, large expressive brown eyes, long dark wavy hair worn loose over her
shoulders, small gold pendant necklace, light natural makeup. She wears a soft
blush-pink wrap blouse and dark comfortable pants with simple flat sandals —
dressed up nicely for a romantic holiday evening at home.

Layout: top row — full-body views standing: front, three-quarter, side
profile, back. Bottom row — five head-and-shoulders expression studies:
1) excited anticipation with hands clasped under chin, 2) hopeful peeking with
raised eyebrows, 3) confused disappointment, 4) hurt sadness with glistening
eyes, 5) cold anger with crossed arms and one raised eyebrow.

Clean character-sheet layout, soft studio lighting, sharp 4K detail, no text
labels.
```

## פרומפט B — לוקיישן-שיט: בית + חצר + מלונה + כלב

```
Pixar-style 3D animated location and character reference sheet, matching the
rendering style of the attached reference sheet. Subject: a modest
single-story Israeli suburban home at night — warm yellow light glowing from
the windows, a front door with a small porch, potted plants, low stone fence,
and a small backyard with trimmed grass. In the backyard stands a classic
wooden doghouse with a slanted shingle roof, large enough for a big dog. The
family dog: a big friendly golden-brown mixed-breed with floppy ears, shaggy
fur, and a red collar.

Layout: large top panel — wide view of the house front at night under a full
moon with deep-blue sky. Middle panels — backyard view with the doghouse,
close-up of the doghouse entrance. Bottom row — the dog in three poses
(sitting, lying down, curled up asleep) and three face studies (happy with
tongue out, curious head-tilt, sleepy).

Warm streetlight and moonlight palette, fireflies, clean reference-sheet
layout, sharp 4K detail, no text labels.
```
