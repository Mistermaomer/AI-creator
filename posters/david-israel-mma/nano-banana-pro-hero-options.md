## 1. Dark Arena (עריכה של Image 1)

```

KEEP PIXEL-IDENTICAL: his face, expression, head angle, hair, beard, skin texture, body, tattoos, pose and the camera distance. Do not beautify, slim, sharpen or repaint his face.

CHANGE ONLY:
1. Wardrobe: add black boxing hand wraps on both hands. Keep him shirtless as in the photo.
2. Background: replace it with the same studio at night – black heavy bags on chains fading into darkness, faint city lights through the big windows far behind, thin volumetric haze.
3. Lighting: keep the existing light on his face, add a strong blood-red rim light from the top-right on his shoulder and arm and a cool white rim light from the top-left, deep black shadows, a light sheen of sweat on his shoulders.
4. Grade: desaturated, high contrast, cinematic, fine 35mm film grain.

Output: portrait 4:5, 2K, photoreal, real-camera look, no plastic smoothing, no text, no logos.
```

```
Create ONE photoreal cinematic photo of David, an MMA coach, built from the two reference photos.

IDENTITY LOCK (most important): David's face must match Image 1 and Image 2 exactly – same eyes, brows, nose, lips, beard shape and density, hairline, curly dark hair, skin tone, calm intense expression, mouth closed. Do not beautify, slim, age or de-age him, do not invent a new face. Keep his face large enough in the frame to be clearly recognizable.

SCENE: He walks straight toward the camera down the center of his studio at night, between two rows of black heavy bags hanging on chains, mid-stride, one foot forward. Plain fitted black t-shirt (no logo), black training pants, black hand wraps, a red focus mitt hanging from each hand at his sides. Behind him, the big windows show faint city lights. Thin haze in the air. CAMERA: low angle from knee height looking slightly up, 35mm lens, full body in frame with headroom, shallow depth of field so the bags blur toward the edges, he is tack sharp. Slight motion blur only on his back foot.

REALISM: real-camera look – natural skin texture, fine 35mm film grain, deep contrast, desaturated grade with red accents, no plastic smoothing, no text, no logos, no other people.

Output: portrait 4:5, 2K.
```
